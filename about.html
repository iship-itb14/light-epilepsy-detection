About us

Three graduate students in Neural Networks and Deep Learning at the University of Colorado Boulder, Fall 2026. Select a portrait to read about each member's contribution and background.

Sejal Hukare

Led the technical work: data pipeline, model development, evaluation and this website.

Portfolio GitHub LinkedIn

Contribution

Designed and implemented the full pipeline, from the selection and cleaning of 14 CHB-MIT patients to patient-wise splits and leave-one-patient-out evaluation. Trained and evaluated the baseline CNN and the lightweight EEGNet, and carried out the follow-up studies on split leakage, channel reduction, int8 quantization and pruning. Also designed and built this website and kept the decision log that records each methodological choice.

Author of four peer-reviewed conference and journal papers.

Python, TensorFlow/Keras, scikit-learn, SQL, Streamlit, Tableau, Grafana, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
